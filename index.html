The problem

Most people decisions fail for the same three reasons.

Strong CVs, wrong fit.

Credentials and interview performance tell you what someone has done. They say very little about how they will work, what will motivate them, and where they will derail.

Decisions made in isolation.

Hiring, development and promotion are usually run by different people using different criteria. Nobody can compare a candidate, a high-potential and a successor on the same terms.

Reports that move nothing.

Most assessment output ends up as a document nobody acts on, so the decision still gets made on instinct.

We built BA Talent to close that gap.

Method

Four steps from question to action.

Define success.

We agree in writing what a strong outcome looks like for this role, team or decision.

Assess against it.

Candidates or employees complete a behavioural assessment. We analyse the results against the agreed profile.

Make it comparable.

You receive a structured view that lets you compare options on the same terms, with strengths, risks and evidence.

Act on it.

A shortlist and interview guide, a 90-day plan, a team action plan, or a development roadmap, depending on the decision.

Human judgement stays in the room. Assessment informs the decision. It does not replace the interview, the references, or the manager.

FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

Are these personality tests?

No. Harrison Assessments measures work-related behavioural tendencies across 175 traits and compares them against the specific demands of a role or team. It is a decision aid, not a label.

Will this replace our interviews?

No. Assessment tells you where to probe. Your interviews, references and job evidence remain central to the decision.

Who sees the results?

Only the people you designate. We agree data handling, access and retention in writing before an engagement begins, and we work within Ethiopian data protection requirements.

How long does an engagement take?

It depends on scope. A screening shortlist can be delivered in around two weeks. A team review or succession map is scoped over a longer period. We confirm timelines before we start.

What does an engagement cost?

We scope each engagement to the decision you are making: the role, the team size, and the depth required. We discuss the commercial structure on our first call.
